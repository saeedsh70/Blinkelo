# 💜 Support Blinkelo

Blinkelo and Blinkelo Link are free, open source, ad-free and collect no data. They are built by one developer in their spare time. If you enjoy them, a donation of any size helps pay for the time that goes into new features, fixes and support.

Donations are accepted in crypto only. Scan a QR code with your wallet or copy the address.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Send each coin **only on the network shown next to it**. Coins sent on a different network (for example USDT on Ethereum/ERC20 to the TRC20 address) can't be recovered.

## Other ways to help (free)

- ⭐ **Star the repository** — it helps other people find Blinkelo.
- 📣 **Share it**: post a screenshot or a short clip of your setup and tag the project.
- 🐛 **Report bugs** and suggest features in [Issues](https://github.com/saeedsh70/Blinkelo/issues).
- 🌍 **Translate** Blinkelo into your language (see [Contributing](README.md#-contributing)).

Thank you for supporting independent open source software! 😉
